# Just Volleyball

Just Volleyball is an arcade volleyball game by temkosoft, released on Steam for Windows PC on November 16, 2026. Move your player manually, aim contacts with the cursor and build a rally through receiving, setting and attacking.

[justvolleyball.app](https://justvolleyball.app/) is an independent fan site for players who want a new look, a clearer understanding of the controls or a game with friends.

## Build your player

A player appearance combines face features, body proportions, hair, uniform colors, jersey number and accessories. The [Shop](https://justvolleyball.app/characters/shop/) connects accessory browsing and item try-on with the Locker. The [customization guide](https://justvolleyball.app/customization/) explains the available parts.

An appearance code describes a combination of items. It does not grant ownership of accessories or change your game account. Check a look’s requirements before importing it.

## Start playing

Read the [Beginner guide](https://justvolleyball.app/guides/beginner/) and check your active [Controls](https://justvolleyball.app/guides/controls/). Practice a controlled receive toward a teammate, then add a set and an attack. Local play and bot sessions give you room to repeat movements before joining public matches.

The game supports formats from 1v1 to 6v6, online and offline play, customizable players and replay tools. Use a format that suits your group and agree on who covers the next contact.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this the official game website?

This is an independent fan site. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by temkosoft or Valve. Purchases and game-account services are handled by the relevant providers.

### Are named skins an official character roster?

The game uses customizable avatars. Community look names describe player-created interpretations. Check the outfit image and required accessories on each detail page.

### How do I import a character code?

Copy the complete code, open the in-game Locker, choose Presets and select Import. Review the appearance and missing items before confirming. The [import guide](https://justvolleyball.app/character-codes/how-to-import/) also covers player-card pictures.

### Where are my saved web Locker presets?

They are stored in the current browser on your device. Export looks you want to retain before clearing site storage or switching browsers.

### Can I play the demo today?

The November Encore Demo was a limited event. Check the official Steam listing for a current demo download option.

### How do I report a problem?

Email support@justvolleyball.app with the affected page, the action you attempted and what happened. For game compatibility issues, include the edition and version. Never send passwords or authentication codes.
